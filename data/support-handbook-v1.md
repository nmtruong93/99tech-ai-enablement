# Customer Support Handbook

Version 1.3 · Last updated 1 March 2025

## Response times

- Reply to every new ticket within 4 hours.
- Use the customer's first name and keep a friendly tone.

## Withdrawals

- Withdrawals are processed within 48 hours.
- If a customer says a withdrawal is late, check the payments dashboard. If it's not there, check the provider portal.
- Tell the customer the money should arrive within 3–5 days.

## Refunds

- Agents can issue refunds up to $200 at their discretion, for example for game errors or as a goodwill gesture.
- Anything above $200 goes to the team lead.

## Verification (KYC)

- Customers must be verified before their first withdrawal.
- If verification fails, explain what was wrong with the documents so the customer can resubmit.

## Escalation

Escalate to the team lead:

- Threats of legal action
- Requests to speak to a manager
