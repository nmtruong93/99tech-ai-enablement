# Customer Support Handbook

Version 2.0 · Effective 15 August 2026

## Response times

- First reply target: 1 hour from ticket creation.
- Reply in the customer's language where possible.
- Don't promise dates or outcomes you can't verify.

## Withdrawals

- Withdrawals from verified customers are processed within 24 hours, then take 1–3 business days to arrive, depending on the provider.
- Statuses: `pending` (not yet processed), `processing` (sent to provider), `completed` (provider confirmed), `rejected` (blocked by us).
- A withdrawal still `pending` after 24 hours, or `processing` after 3 business days, goes to the Payments team.

## Deposits

- A `failed` deposit is never credited. If the customer was charged, the provider reverses it to the source account, usually within 3–5 business days.

## Refunds

- Agents can issue refunds up to USD 100.
- Refunds above USD 100 need team lead approval before they are issued.
- Every refund needs a reason and the ticket ID.

## Verification (KYC)

- Customers under KYC review cannot withdraw until the review completes.
- Never share the review reason or what our checks found. Tell the customer the review is in progress and that we'll contact them if we need anything.

## Bonuses

- Bonus questions are answered from the current Bonus Terms. Don't make exceptions to the terms.

## Privacy

- Never discuss another customer's account, even with family members or people who say they share the account.
- Never paste customer contact details or documents into chat channels.

## Escalation

Escalate to the team lead and don't resolve the ticket yourself:

- Legal threats or chargeback threats
- Any mention of gambling harm, losing control, or wanting to stop playing. Also follow the self-exclusion process.
- Suspected fraud or account takeover
- Anything you're not sure about
