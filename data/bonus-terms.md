# Bonus Terms

Effective 1 June 2026

## Welcome bonus

- 100% of the first deposit, up to USD 100.
- Bonus funds are credited with the deposit and can't be withdrawn until the wagering requirement is met.
- Wagering requirement: 30 times the bonus amount, within 14 days of the bonus being credited.
- Only slot games count fully towards wagering. Table games count 10%. Live casino doesn't count.
- Maximum bet while a bonus is active: USD 5 per spin or round.

## Withdrawing with an active bonus

- If a customer withdraws before meeting the wagering requirement, the bonus and any winnings from it are forfeited.
- If the 14 days pass before the requirement is met, the bonus and any winnings from it are removed.

## Exceptions

None. Support agents can't change bonus terms or credit bonuses manually.
