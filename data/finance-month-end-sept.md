# Finance — September month-end notes

Prepared 28 Sep 2026 · Internal

## Summary

- Net gaming revenue was 4% above forecast.
- Payment processing costs were up 11% on August, mainly from QuickPay fees after the August price change.
- Marketing spend is in line with budget.

## Payment providers

- **PayNova:** normal operation all month.
- **VietPay:** normal operation. 23 deposits failed on bank timeouts; all were auto-reversed by the provider.
- **QuickPay:** settlement incident from 20 to 24 Sep. 312 withdrawals were delayed by 2 to 5 days. 298 have settled. 14 are still `pending` on our side and under investigation with QuickPay. Payments team owns the follow-up; expected resolution by 2 Oct.

## Refunds

- Support refunds in September: USD 14,820 across 96 refunds.
- USD 6,200 of that exceeded the agent limit in the support handbook without recorded approval.
- Several refunds had no ticket reference.

## Chargebacks

- 9 chargebacks received in September (August: 4). 5 followed support tickets that were still unanswered after 24 hours.

## Actions

- Payments team to close out the remaining QuickPay withdrawals.
- Support to tighten refund approvals. Finance will audit October refunds.
